How a deal earns its badge

  1. Found, not invented. Every product, price and store link below was read from the store or from a deals source that names the store, on 27 Nov 2026. Nothing here is a guess.
  2. True low. The sale price is the lowest ever reported for that item — below every earlier sale we could find, including Prime Day and last Black Friday.
  3. Strong price. A cut of roughly a quarter or more against the usual price, at or near the item's recent low. Worth buying if you want it.
  4. Routine sale. A small cut, or a big percentage measured against a reference price the item rarely sold at. The badge says so instead of dressing it up.
  5. Checked, then dated. Each card names its source and the sweep time. A price from this morning can be gone by tonight — that is why the check time is on every card.
